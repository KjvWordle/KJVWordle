How to play

Guess the word in six tries

Each day there is one hidden five-letter word from the King James Bible. Everyone gets the same word.

  • Each guess must be a five-letter word from the KJV word list.
  • Press Enter (↵) to submit a guess. The tiles change colour to show how close you were.
  • You can tap any tile in the current row to jump to it and fill or fix that letter.
  • A new puzzle appears at midnight, your local time.

Questions

What kinds of words come up?
Any five-letter word from the King James text. That includes everyday words, archaic forms like doeth and durst, and names of people and places like Caleb and Joppa.
What about repeated letters?
A letter is only marked orange as many times as it appears in the answer. Extra copies are shown dark.
Where are my stats kept?
Your games are saved in your browser. When you finish a puzzle, an anonymous result (won or lost, and how many guesses) is added to the community stats on the dashboard. Nothing that identifies you is collected.
